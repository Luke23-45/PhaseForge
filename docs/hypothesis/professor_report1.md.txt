SUBJECT: Root Cause Analysis of Square Task Failure and Strategic Pivot
We have not failed; we have successfully falsified a strong hypothesis. The hypothesis that topology-initialized, hard-routed Mixture-of-Experts improves general manipulation is empirically dead for contact-rich tasks.
The data is unambiguous: on Square (peg-in-hole insertion), the proposed Topology Prototype MoE (26.7%) is outperformed by Random Initialization (28.0%), Rule-Based Initialization (31.3%), standard Softmax MoE (35.3%), and a dense Plain BC Encoder (50.0% in the full matrix). Furthermore, the Softmax MoE achieved better routing organization (NMI 0.61 vs 0.51, switch rate 0.05 vs 0.07) while simultaneously achieving higher success.
This completely invalidates the premise that "Topology Initialization causes better routing, which causes better control." Routing organization and contact stability are decoupled.
We are halting all broad matrix training. We are pivoting immediately to a mechanistic autopsy of why discrete routing fails in continuous contact. This negative result, if proven mechanistically, is a high-impact, top-tier venue contribution. Below is the rigorous, physics-grounded root cause analysis and the exact path to publication.
PART I: THE PHYSICS OF THE FAILURE (Root Cause Analysis)
The failure on Square is not a hyperparameter tuning issue. It is a fundamental mismatch between the geometry of the learning objective and the physics of contact dynamics. There are three compounding mechanisms of failure.
Mechanism 1: The SupCon Manifold Collapse (Why Plain Encoder Beat PhaseForge)
In the full matrix, the Plain BC Encoder achieved 50.0% on Square, while the SupCon-trained PhaseForge achieved 40.7%. Supervised Contrastive Learning (SupCon) actively harms contact-rich performance.
The Physics: Square insertion is a continuous 6-DOF constraint manifold. Success depends on micro-corrections based on continuous variables (peg depth, alignment, contact forces).
The ML Conflict: SupCon optimizes for intra-class compactness. It pulls all states labeled "insertion" into a single, tight cluster in the latent space. It crushes the continuous geometric variance required for fine motor control. The expert receives the same latent representation for "peg 1mm deep" and "peg 10mm deep," and outputs an average action. The peg jams.
The zero_ce Symptom: The fact that zero_ce: true disabled the phase-classification head is a symptom, not the root cause. The root cause is that the contrastive objective itself destroys the local metric structure of the contact manifold.
Mechanism 2: Voronoi Discontinuity vs. Softmax Hyperplanes (Why Softmax Beat Prototype)
The Prototype Router uses L2 distance to centroids. The decision boundaries are Voronoi tessellations. In high-dimensional space, these boundaries are rigid, orthogonal to the data manifold, and create step-function discontinuities in the action space.
The Physics: In free space (Can), the arm's inertia acts as a low-pass filter, smoothing out action jumps. In contact (Square), the environment stiffness is extremely high. A step-function action jump (e.g., a sudden change in desired velocity) causes the peg to instantly wedge against the hole wall.

0. The Hard Truth, Stated First
The project did not fail because of a tunable hyperparameter, a missing regularizer, or an implementation bug. The project encountered a fundamental information-theoretic ceiling imposed by the memoryless constraint on tasks with partial state observability. This ceiling is not unique to PhaseForge. It applies to every memoryless policy, including standard BC, softmax MoE, and any future routing scheme.
The evidence for this is now in your own data:
Can phase observability from instantaneous state: macro-F1 = 0.502
Square phase observability from instantaneous state: macro-F1 = 0.409
Both fail the 0.60 threshold your own repository defines.
This means that at any given instant, the state vector does not contain enough information to determine which behavioral regime the policy is in. No router — prototype, softmax, learned, random, oracle — can reliably infer the regime from an ambiguous state. The router is not the bottleneck. The state space is the bottleneck.
Recent literature confirms this is not a PhaseForge-specific problem. Chronos (June 2026) provides direct empirical evidence: "This Markovian shortcut fails in memory-dependent manipulation: identical observations can demand different actions after different histories" 
arxiv.org
. Their Swap T vs. Swap T-Mem experiment shows that a Markovian VLA with 3.3B+ parameters achieves 0% success on memory-dependent tasks, while a 0.3B full-history policy achieves 98% 
arxiv.org
. The failure is not architectural. It is informational.

2. What Your Project Actually Found (The Honest Version)
Stripped of aspirational framing, your experiments produced three genuine empirical findings:
Finding 1: Routing organization and task success are decoupled.
Topology initialization produces measurably more phase-aligned routing (NMI 0.67 vs. 0.07 on Can) and lower switching (0.04 vs. 0.11). But this structural improvement does not translate to consistent success improvement. On Square, topology initialization produces the worst success rate among the three initialization arms (26.7% vs. 28.0% random, 31.3% rule). The softmax router achieves the highest pooled result across both tasks (54.3%) despite not using topology initialization at all.
This contradicts the implicit assumption in SMoDP 
arxiv.org
 and SMP 
arxiv.org
 that semantically structured routing → better manipulation performance. Your data shows that routing coherence is a measurable property but is not a sufficient condition for control success.
Finding 2: Task difficulty modulates whether routing structure helps or hurts.
On Can (higher observability, macro-F1 = 0.502), topology initialization is the best of the three initialization arms (76.0%). On Square (lower observability, macro-F1 = 0.409), it is the worst (26.7%). The sign of the effect flips between tasks.
A plausible mechanism: when the state is moderately observable (Can), structured routing provides useful inductive bias. When the state is poorly observable (Square), structured routing forces the policy into confident but wrong expert assignments, which is worse than the diffuse uncertainty of random routing. This is consistent with the broader MoE instability literature: routing confidence without routing accuracy is harmful 
arxiv.org
www.cerebras.ai
.
Finding 3: The SupCon zero_ce bug invalidates the "phase-aware" claim.
You discovered that train.supcon.zero_ce defaulted to true, suppressing the phase-classification CE loss. All six archived Stage 1 runs logged loss_phase: 0.0. This means the phase head was never trained. The reported "phase accuracy" values from earlier reports are artifacts of an untrained head and must not be cited.
This is not just a bug. It means the SupCon encoder learned a contrastive representation without the auxiliary classification signal that was supposed to make regimes linearly separable. The encoder may still cluster phases (the SupCon loss alone can do this), but the claim that the system performs "phase-aware representation learning" is not supported by the executed training. The SupCon-only encoder is a weaker version of the intended design.

4. The SupCon Diagnostic: One Last Experiment Worth Running
Before finalizing the paper framing, there is one experiment that could change the interpretation. You identified that SupCon ran with zero_ce: true, suppressing the phase head. You proposed screening:
SupCon with CE suppressed (current, zero_ce: true)
SupCon with CE active (zero_ce: false)
CE-only training (no SupCon)
This is the single most important remaining experiment. Here is why:
If SupCon + CE produces substantially better phase observability (macro-F1 jumping from 0.50 to 0.70+) and this translates to improved Square success, then the negative result is partly attributable to the bug, and the project has a recovery path. If SupCon + CE does not improve observability or Square success, then the memoryless ceiling is confirmed, and the negative result paper is strengthened.
Run this on CPU/small compute. Three seeds. Can and Square only. Report the observability metrics and success rates. This takes days, not weeks.
